Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

In short: our games do not ask for, collect, or sell your personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your own device.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Coral Bay and any other mobile apps or games published by Just Botond (Botond, Braşov, Romania) on Google Play (together, the "Apps"). "We", "us" and "our" refer to Just Botond.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment details through the Apps.

  • Game data on your device: settings and saved progress are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us.
  • No accounts: the Apps do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • No advertising or tracking: the Apps do not show ads and do not use advertising identifiers or third-party tracking tools.

3. Information collected by Google

The Apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may collect technical data such as anonymous crash reports, app performance statistics, and installation counts, and shares aggregated, non-identifying statistics with developers through the Google Play Console. This processing is done by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy. We use this aggregated information only to fix bugs and improve the Apps.

4. How we use information

The only information available to us is the aggregated, anonymous statistics described above. We use it solely to keep the Apps stable and improve gameplay. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

5. Permissions

The Apps only request the device permissions needed for the game to work. They do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or location.

6. Children's privacy

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App. If you have contacted us by email, you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

8. Your rights (EU / GDPR)

If you are in the European Union or EEA, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data about you, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Since we do not hold personal data from the Apps, most requests will be answered by confirming this. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the Apps and any correspondence you send us. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.
